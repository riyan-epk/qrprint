How it works

From phone to paper in four steps.

No queue at the counter, no files sent over WhatsApp, no USB drives. The customer does it all from their phone.

  1. 1

    Scan the QR

    The customer scans the QR code on your counter. Your shop's print page opens straight away — no app, no sign-up.

  2. 2

    Add files or photos

    Several PDFs, Word files or pictures at once — or a CNIC front and back. Pick pages, turn them, and see the price up front.

  3. 3

    Choose & pay

    Copies, colour, single or double-sided and page range. Pay online with Safepay, or in cash at the counter.

  4. 4

    Collect the pages

    The job prints on your printer by itself, and the customer watches the status live on their phone.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and your shop's print page opens in the browser. Nothing to download and no account to create.

Which files can customers print?

PDF, Word (.doc, .docx), Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, RTF and text files, plus JPG and PNG photos. The default limit is 25 MB per file, and you can change it in your settings.

Can customers print a CNIC copy or passport photos?

Yes. For an ID card copy they take a photo of the front and back, line each one up with the card frame, and both sides print on one page at real card size. For passport photos they choose 4 to 20 photos of 35 × 45 mm on one sheet, with cutting lines.

Can customers send several files at once?

Yes — up to 20 files in one order. They can choose which pages to print, rotate pages, and change the order. With double-sided printing, each file starts on a new sheet.

What do I need in my shop?

A printer, a Windows or Linux computer connected to it, and an internet connection. We set up the print agent on that computer and give you your QR code and dashboard login.

How do I get paid?

In cash at the counter, or online through your own Safepay merchant account. Print payments go directly to you. JazzCash wallet payments are coming soon.

What happens if a print fails?

If the printer jams or runs out of paper, the job is flagged on your dashboard. You can reprint it once the printer is ready, or refund it. The customer sees the status on their phone the whole time.

Are customers' files kept private?

Yes. Files are deleted automatically as soon as they print. Uploads that are never paid for are removed within 30 minutes, and job history is cleared after 7 days.

Can it run without anyone at the counter?

Yes. Kiosk mode is made for unattended use with online payment — if a job can't print, the customer's payment is refunded automatically.
